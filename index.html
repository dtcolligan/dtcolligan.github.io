Told or Enforced

A pre-registered study of when telling an agent the rules substitutes for enforcing them. Eight models, four open-weight families, a stronger and a weaker sibling in each. Telling raises the first-action safe rate in every family, but enforcement still adds on top of telling, and it is nearly all of the barrier in the weak families.

First author. Preprint coming.

39 tasks over an agent harness, with a deterministic offline grader and every trajectory released. Built to separate two things that usually get measured together: what the model was told, and what the runtime would actually let it do.
