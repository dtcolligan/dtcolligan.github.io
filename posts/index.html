    ECBD: a review

    GDPval: a review

    A review of OpenAI's GDPval. The occupation selection is careful and the cost analysis is the best idea in the paper, but the rubrics were never run against themselves: tracing the gold taskset on HuggingFace turned up 4 bugs in 15 tasks.
